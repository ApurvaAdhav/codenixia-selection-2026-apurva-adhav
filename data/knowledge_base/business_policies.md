# Business Policies

## Anomaly Response Policy
When an anomaly or a significant sales drop (>=10%) is detected for a
Region+Product combination:
1. Within 24 hours, the Regional Sales Manager must be notified.
2. Check for known causes first: stockouts, logistics delays, pricing
   errors, or competitor promotions in that region.
3. If no obvious cause is found within 3 business days, escalate to the
   Supply Chain and Quality teams for a joint review.
4. Document the finding in the Incident Report log, even if the cause
   turns out to be benign (e.g., a one-off large return).

## Discounting Policy
Regional managers may approve discounts up to 10% without escalation.
Discounts between 10-25% require Sales Director approval. Anything above
25% requires VP approval and must be logged with a business justification.

## Forecast Usage Policy
Sales forecasts generated by the internal forecasting tool are intended for
directional planning (staffing, inventory ordering) and should be treated
as indicative, not committed targets. Forecasts with an R-squared below 0.3
should be treated as low-confidence and cross-checked with the regional
sales team before being used for inventory decisions.

## Underperforming Product Review
Any product that shows a sustained revenue decline of 15% or more over two
consecutive reporting periods (60 days total) is automatically flagged for
a Quarterly Business Review discussion, covering: continued investment,
price adjustment, or discontinuation.

## Data Quality Policy
Sales data uploaded to the BI system must include Date, Region, Product,
Category, Customer Segment, Quantity, Revenue, and Cost at minimum. Rows
with missing Date, Region, Product, Quantity, or Revenue are excluded from
analysis rather than estimated, to avoid introducing bias into KPI
reporting.
