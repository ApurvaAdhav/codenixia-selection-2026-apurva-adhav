# Previous Incident Reports

## Incident 2023-11: Region West / Product A Supply Delay
In November 2023, Product A sales in Region West dropped approximately 30%
over a 3-week period. Root cause was identified as a delayed shipment from
the primary supplier, which caused intermittent stockouts at regional
distribution centers. Resolution: switched to a secondary supplier for
Region West, with a 2-week lead time buffer added going forward. This is
the most common historical cause of Product A / Region West underperformance.

## Incident 2024-02: Product C Pricing Error
A pricing configuration error caused Product C to be listed 20% above the
intended price for 10 days in the Corporate segment. This caused a
temporary revenue dip in that segment despite steady unit interest. Once
corrected, sales recovered within a week. Lesson learned: pricing changes
now require a second approver before going live.

## Incident 2024-05: Region South Seasonal Dip
Region South consistently sees a 10-15% dip in Home & Kitchen category
sales during the two weeks around major regional holidays, due to store
closures and reduced foot traffic. This is considered normal seasonal
variation, not an anomaly requiring escalation, and is now factored into
regional forecasting expectations.

## Incident 2024-08: Product A Quality Complaint Spike
A batch of Product A units shipped with a minor packaging defect led to a
spike in returns and a short-term sales dip in the Consumer segment across
multiple regions, most visible in Region West given its higher Product A
volume. Resolution: the affected batch was recalled and quality control
checkpoints were added at the packaging stage. Region West has historically
been the most sensitive region to Product A supply and quality issues due
to its concentration of Product A sales volume.
